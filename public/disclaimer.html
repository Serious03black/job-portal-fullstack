Corporate Disclaimer & Recruitment Fraud Alert

Important Public Advisory | AXYTES Solutions Private Limited

⚠️ NOTICE TO ALL JOB APPLICANTS: ZERO FEE POLICY

AXYTES Solutions Private Limited DOES NOT CHARGE ANY MONETARY FEES, REGISTRATION DEPOSITS, LAPTOP SECURITY CHARGES, OR INTERVIEW FEES FROM JOB APPLICANTS AT ANY STAGE OF THE RECRUITMENT PROCESS.

1. Beware of Fraudulent Job Offers

It has come to our attention that unscrupulous individuals or fake recruitment agencies may impersonate AXYTES executives, issuing fake interview appointment letters or demanding payment into personal UPI / bank accounts.

Please note that:

  • All official recruitment emails from our company are sent strictly from domain addresses ending in @axytes.in. We never send official offer letters or interview confirmations from free webmail accounts like Gmail, Yahoo, or Outlook.
  • We never demand security deposits for interview scheduling, laptop delivery, or training kits.
  • If anyone approaches you demanding money pretending to represent AXYTES, report it immediately to fraud-alert@axytes.in and lodge a complaint with your local cyber crime authorities.
