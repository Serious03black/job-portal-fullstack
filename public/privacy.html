Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024 | AXYTES Solutions Private Limited

At AXYTES Solutions Private Limited ("AXYTES", "we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected through our website (www.axytes.in), Candidate Portal, Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal, and Admin recruitment applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to facilitate recruitment, staffing, payroll administration, and candidate placements. This includes:

  • Candidate Information: Name, contact details, email, resumes/CVs, educational credentials, work experience history, previous compensation, expected salary, and preferred work locations.
  • Employee / Associate Information: Government ID credentials (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport), bank account details for payroll disbursal, PF (UAN) numbers, ESIC insurance identifiers, attendance logs, and leave records.
  • Client & Employer Data: Company name, official GSTIN, authorized contact persons, billing address, hiring mandates, and vacancy specifications.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, session timestamps, and device details captured automatically for security auditing and platform performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Personal data collected by AXYTES is utilized strictly for legitimate business and recruitment operations:

  • Assessing candidate qualifications and matching them with relevant client job openings.
  • Transmitting candidate profiles to prospective client hiring managers with candidate consent.
  • Processing payroll, monthly salary disbursements, tax deductions (TDS), and statutory remittances (EPF, ESI, Professional Tax).
  • Maintaining statutory employer-employee records as mandated by Indian Labour Laws.
  • Preventing recruitment fraud, unauthorized access, and maintaining portal security.

3. Data Confidentiality & Protection

AXYTES enforces strict administrative, technical, and physical security standards to guard against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data. Candidate contact information is never sold, leased, or traded to third-party marketing brokers.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your data with:

  • Verified Client Employers: Solely for the purpose of candidate evaluation and interview scheduling.
  • Statutory Authorities: Income Tax Department, EPFO, ESIC, and labour regulatory bodies as required by applicable Indian laws.
  • Banking Partners: For direct NEFT/RTGS salary processing and insurance providers for mediclaim coverage.

5. Data Retention & Candidate Rights

Candidate profiles are retained in our active recruitment pool to notify candidates of future matching career opportunities. Candidates have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of their profile from our database by emailing privacy@axytes.in.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Our platform uses session cookies to maintain secure authenticated logins for Job Seekers, Deputed Employees, and Administrators. You can manage or disable cookies via your browser preferences, though some portal features may function sub-optimally.
